# Get started

Three short pages get you from no account to finding your way around the creator.

| Page | You learn |
|---|---|
| [Open the creator](open-the-creator.md) | Make an account, open the creator, start or import a game, and how saving works |
| [The workspace](workspace.md) | The tool bar, docked tools, layouts, the command palette, undo and the tour |
| [On a phone](phone.md) | One tool at a time, the tool bar along the bottom, and touch controls in Play |

Then build your first real game in the [tutorial](../tutorial/index.md).

The creator makes two kinds of games. **Bitsy** games have rooms, characters
to talk to and stories. **Chili Beat** games are made of things that bump,
move and change on the beat, with rules as cards. New game asks which one you
want. See [Chili Beat](../beat/index.md) and its [Bee Home tutorial](../beat/tutorial.md).
