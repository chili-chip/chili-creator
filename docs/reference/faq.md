# FAQ

## Making games

??? question "Do I need to know how to code?"
    No. You draw, place things and write dialog. [Dialog scripting](../editors/bitsy/dialog-scripting.md) is there when you want choices and variables, and the **Insert** menu and blocks in [Dialog](../editors/bitsy/tools/dialog.md) write it for you.

??? question "Which editor should I use?"
    The [Bitsy editor](../editors/bitsy/index.md) for stories with characters to talk to. The [Chili Beat editor](../editors/beat/index.md) for puzzles, chases and toys where things move on their own. See [Editors](../editors/index.md).

??? question "Can I move a game to another editor?"
    No. Each editor makes its own kind of game, and a game always opens in the editor that made it.

??? question "Can I open a game I made in the Bitsy editor?"
    Yes. In [Projects](../get-started/projects.md), choose **Import** and pick the `.bitsy` file or the `.html` page you exported from Bitsy.

??? question "Can I take my game back to the Bitsy editor?"
    Yes. [Download](../editors/bitsy/tools/download.md) the `.bitsy` file and open it at [make.bitsy.org](https://make.bitsy.org).

??? question "Why does my edit not show in Play?"
    Play takes a copy of the game when it starts. Choose **Restart** in [Play](../editors/bitsy/tools/play.md) to see your change.

??? question "Why does my sprite say nothing?"
    Check that the sprite has dialog in [Dialog](../editors/bitsy/tools/dialog.md), and look at [Problems](../editors/bitsy/tools/problems.md). A dialog that points at a deleted room or item shows there.

## Saving and limits

??? question "Where are my games saved?"
    When you are signed in, on your Chili account. The creator saves as you work, and the top bar says **Saving…**, **Saved** or **Not saved**. When you are not signed in, games stay in this browser only.

??? question "How many games can I keep?"
    30, released games included. One game can be up to 1.5 MB. See [Projects](../get-started/projects.md#limits).

??? question "How do I keep a backup?"
    [Download](../editors/bitsy/tools/download.md) the `.bitsy` file now and then.

## Releasing

??? question "Why can I not release my game?"
    Releasing needs a verified email address. Open the link in the email Chili Platform sent when you signed up.

??? question "Do players see my edits straight away?"
    No. Players keep the version you released. Choose **Publish update** in [Projects](../get-started/projects.md#publish-an-update) to send them your edits.

??? question "How do I sell my game?"
    Release it, then list it from your Library on Chili Platform. See [List a game for sale](https://platform.chilichip.eu/help/sell-a-game).

## VGC Zero

??? question "How do I play my game on the console?"
    Copy the `.bitsy` file into `bitsy-games` on the CHILICHIP drive. See [Play on VGC Zero](vgc-zero.md).

??? question "Why does the text break into pages in other places on the console?"
    citsy's dialog box has three lines, Bitsy's has two. See [Differences from Bitsy](../editors/bitsy/differences.md).
